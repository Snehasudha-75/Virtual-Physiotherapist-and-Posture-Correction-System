Exercise Steps

{% if pain_level == 1 %}
  • Stand Upright – Keep your spine straight as shown in the GIF
  • Pull Shoulders Back – Retract both shoulders fully
  • Squeeze Shoulder Blades – Hold the squeeze firmly
  • Keep Chin Neutral – Avoid bending your neck
  • Hold 20–30 Seconds – Maintain posture
  • Release Slowly – Return to relaxed position
{% elif pain_level == 2 %}
  • Relax Your Body – Start gently
  • Slow Shoulder Pull – Pull shoulders back halfway
  • Controlled Movement – Follow the GIF pace
  • Neck Relaxed – No stiffness
  • Hold 10–15 Seconds
  • Repeat 5–8 Times
{% elif pain_level == 3 %}
  • Very Gentle Movement – Minimal effort only
  • Partial Shoulder Squeeze – About 30–40%
  • No Force – Pain-free motion only
  • Slow Breathing – Inhale and exhale slowly
  • Hold 5–8 Seconds
  • Rest Between Reps
{% endif %}
💡 Pro Tip: {% if pain_level == 1 %}

For best results, hold this posture for 15-30 seconds. Repeat 10-15 times daily. Focus on keeping your shoulders down to avoid tension in your neck. This preventive exercise helps maintain good posture long-term.

{% elif pain_level == 2 %}

Do this exercise 3-5 times daily, 8-10 repetitions each time. Stop if you feel sharp pain. Consistency is more important than intensity. Consider doing these exercises after a warm-up or light stretching.

{% elif pain_level == 3 %}

Take it slowly and gently. Do only 3-5 repetitions, 2 times daily. If you experience pain, stop immediately. It's okay to do partial movements. Healing takes time - be patient with yourself. Consider consulting a physiotherapist.

{% endif %}
