Exercise Instructions

✓ Target Elbow Angle Range

80° – 120°

Maintain this optimal angle to prevent strain and ensure proper preventive exercise form

💡 Pro Tip: {% if pain_level == 1 %}

For best results, position your arm at a 90-degree angle and hold for 10-15 seconds. Repeat 12-15 times daily. Avoid rapid movements to prevent elbow strain.

{% elif pain_level == 2 %}

Do this exercise 4-6 times daily, 8-10 repetitions each time. Hold for 5-10 seconds. Stop if you feel sharp pain. Do light warm-up stretches before starting.

{% elif pain_level == 3 %}

Take it very slowly and gently. Do only 3-5 repetitions, 2 times daily. Hold for 2-5 seconds. If you experience pain, stop immediately. Rest is as important as exercise during recovery.

{% endif %}
