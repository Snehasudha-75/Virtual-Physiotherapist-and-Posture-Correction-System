💡 Pro Tip:

{% if pain_level == 1 %}

For best results, hold this posture for 15-30 seconds. Repeat 10-15 times daily. Focus on keeping your shoulders down to avoid tension in your neck. This preventive exercise helps maintain good posture long-term.

{% elif pain_level == 2 %}

Do this exercise 3-5 times daily, 8-10 repetitions each time. Stop if you feel sharp pain. Consistency is more important than intensity. Consider doing these exercises after a warm-up or light stretching.

{% elif pain_level == 3 %}

Take it slowly and gently. Do only 3-5 repetitions, 2 times daily. If you experience pain, stop immediately. It's okay to do partial movements. Healing takes time - be patient with yourself. Consider consulting a physiotherapist.
